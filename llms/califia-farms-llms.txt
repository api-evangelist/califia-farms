# Califia Farms

> Califia Farms, LLC is a Los Angeles, California based plant-based beverage company founded in 2010 by Greg Steltenpohl. It makes non-dairy oatmilks, almondmilks and organic soymilks, coffee creamers, barista blends, cold brew coffees and teas, and ready-to-drink creamy refreshers, sold through grocery retail, foodservice distribution and direct-to-consumer channels in North America and Europe.
